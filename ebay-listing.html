How it is described. Each stone is described exactly as its original dealer box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is actually in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone arrived with no label at all, that is stated plainly in its entry rather than glossed over.

What has not been done. Nothing has been independently graded, certified or appraised. No origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels, not from a scale or a gauge. Please read the individual entries and judge the photographs for yourself.
