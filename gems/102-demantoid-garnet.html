Catalogue no. 102

Demantoid Garnet — Set of Three Rounds, Chris Johnston Label

Three pale yellow-green rounds with strong fire, labeled to a known cutter.

Three round demantoid garnets in a pale yellowish green, showing the strong brilliance and fire that make demantoid the most prized garnet variety. The label reads “Chris Johnston Demantoid” — a real and recognized name in the demantoid trade, so the label may be citing a cutter or source as a mark of provenance. Like every other attribution in this collection, it comes from the label alone and is not documented. The 0.20 ct figure is written in the singular for what is a box of three stones.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
