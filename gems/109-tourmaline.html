Tourmaline — Labeled Paraiba, Pale Aqua

An oval labeled Paraiba in a pale seafoam aqua, well short of neon saturation.

A single oval tourmaline labeled Paraiba. This deserves a clear caution. Genuine Paraiba is copper-bearing tourmaline prized for an intense neon blue-green, commanding extraordinary prices per carat — and it is also among the most frequently misapplied names in the gem trade, stretched to cover lesser copper-bearing material, other blue-green stones such as apatite, and outright imitations. This stone photographs a pale, light aqua-seafoam, distinctly less saturated than genuine Paraiba's signature color. It is offered as labeled, unverified, and should not be valued at Paraiba prices without a laboratory report.
