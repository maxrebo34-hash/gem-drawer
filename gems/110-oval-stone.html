Oval Stone — Labeled Paraiba, Pale Silvery Grey-Blue

A second Paraiba-labeled stone, this one grey-silver rather than blue-green.

The second stone in this collection labeled Paraiba, and the more pronounced mismatch of the two. Copper-bearing tourmaline produces an intense neon blue to blue-green; this oval photographs a pale grey to silvery blue, outside that color family altogether. Given how large the price gap is between genuine Paraiba and anything else wearing the name, this stone is offered strictly as labeled, unverified, and should not be priced as Paraiba without laboratory confirmation. No weight or measurements given.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
