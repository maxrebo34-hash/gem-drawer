Dark Baguette — Labeled Chrome Tourmaline, Color Unconfirmed

A 10.2 x 6.2 mm fancy cut that photographs near-black, not chrome green.

A baguette or fancy-cut tourmaline measuring 10.2 x 6.2 mm, labeled chrome tourmaline. Stated plainly: chrome tourmaline is prized for a vivid saturated green often compared to emerald or tsavorite, and this stone photographs very dark, close to black, with only a hint of green catching in the facets. That appearance is closer to common black tourmaline — schorl — which is far less valuable. The true body color is best judged in strong direct light, and the stone is offered as labeled with the discrepancy disclosed.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
