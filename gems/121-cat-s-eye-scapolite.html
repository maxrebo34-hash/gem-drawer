Cat's Eye Scapolite — Oval Cabochon, 9–9.9 ct

A taupe cabochon with a clear pale chatoyant band across the dome.

An oval scapolite cabochon in greyish brown-taupe with a pale chatoyant band running across the dome — a genuine cat's-eye effect, clearly visible in the photograph and a good match for its label. Cat's eye scapolite is an uncommon material: scapolite occurs in grey, brown, violet, pink and yellow, and shows chatoyancy only when the right fibrous inclusions are cut correctly as a cabochon. The label gives the weight as a range, 9.00 to 9.90 carats.
