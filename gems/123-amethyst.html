Amethyst — Two Faceted Stones with Gem Data Certificate

A hexagonal step cut and an oval brilliant in medium purple, with certificate.

Two faceted amethysts in a medium purple, one hexagonal step cut and one oval brilliant, accompanied by a leather-bound “Gem Data Certificate” from Nationwide Equity Assets. A word about that certificate: it describes a single 0.50 ct stone, with a tiny rough chip glued into its own display window that is neither of the loose stones here — so its weight and count do not describe what is in this lot. Its physical constants do support genuine amethyst, and it is offered on that basis alone. The “$250 comparative list” figure printed on it is promotional mail-order language, not an appraisal.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
