Catalogue no. 124

Garnet — Two Stones with Gem Data Certificate

One near-black rounded stone and one reddish-pink oval, with certificate.

Two garnets: one dark, nearly black and rounded, one smaller reddish-pink oval. Both colors are plausible for the garnet family — very dark almandine and melanite andradite can read near-black, while the reddish-pink is a more familiar garnet tone. The accompanying Nationwide Equity Assets certificate describes a single 1.36 ct stone rather than these two, so it is useful only as species reference; its specific gravity and refractive index figures sit squarely in garnet's normal range. Its “$250 comparative list” value is promotional language, not an appraisal.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
