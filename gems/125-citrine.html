Catalogue no. 125

Citrine — 0.96 ct Pear, Set in Gem Data Certificate

A golden-yellow pear cut mounted in its own certificate display window.

A light golden to brownish-yellow pear-cut citrine at 0.96 carats, set in the display window of a Nationwide Equity Assets certificate. Unlike the certificate-accompanied amethyst and garnet in this collection, this one has no count discrepancy: the stone photographed is the stone the certificate describes. Its listed constants are consistent with quartz, as expected — citrine and amethyst are the same mineral differing only in trace iron and heat. The “$250 comparative list” figure is promotional, not an appraisal.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
