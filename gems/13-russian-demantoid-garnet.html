Catalogue no. 13

Russian Demantoid Garnet — “Grab Bag” Parcel, 20+ Stones

A bulk parcel of roughly twenty-plus small green garnets plus one rough fragment.

A bulk parcel labeled “Russian Demantoid Grab Bag with Horse Tails” — horsetail inclusions being the fibrous, prized identifying feature associated with genuine Russian demantoid. The box holds approximately twenty or more small round-cut stones ranging from pale yellow-green to olive, plus one larger darker round stone and one irregular rough fragment. No per-stone count or weights were given on the label, as is normal for a parcel sold this way. The label also promises an included jeweler's loupe, which is not present in the box.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
