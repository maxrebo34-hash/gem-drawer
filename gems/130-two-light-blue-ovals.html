Two Light Blue Ovals — Specimen Tray, Matching Pair

A clean matched pair of pale blue ovals, likely topaz or aquamarine.

Two oval-cut stones in a matching light blue, well cut and eye-clean in appearance, from the unlabeled specimen tray. In this pale blue range, blue topaz — extremely common and usually treated — and aquamarine are the obvious candidates, and they are difficult to tell apart visually; a refractometer or specific gravity test settles it. Offered as an unidentified matched pair.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.
