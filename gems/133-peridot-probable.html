Peridot (Probable) — Vivid Yellow-Green Oval

A saturated olive-yellow-green oval — the most confident call in the tray.

A single oval-cut stone in a vivid, saturated yellow-green to olive. This is the most confident identification in the unlabeled specimen tray, for a specific reason: peridot occurs in essentially one color, since its green comes from iron in the crystal structure rather than trace impurities, and this stone sits squarely in that characteristic range. A green sapphire or green tourmaline in a similar tone remains formally possible, and nothing here is certified, so it is offered as probable peridot rather than confirmed.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.
