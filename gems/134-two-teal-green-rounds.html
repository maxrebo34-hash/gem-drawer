Two Teal-Green Rounds — Specimen Tray, Unidentified

Two round stones of different sizes in a medium-deep bluish green.

Two round-cut stones of different sizes, both a medium-deep green with a bluish teal undertone, from the unlabeled specimen tray. Emerald, green tourmaline and tsavorite garnet all appear in this color range. Emerald in particular is among the most imitated and most treated gems on the market and should never be assumed from color alone, so these are offered as unidentified green stones.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
