Catalogue no. 135

Large Deep Green Round — Specimen Tray, Likely Glass or Synthetic

A big, deeply saturated green round that is unusually clean for its size.

A single large round-cut stone in a deep, saturated green with a slight bluish undertone — and offered with a caution rather than an inflated claim. The color is extremely uniform across the whole stone, with no zoning, banding or inclusions of any kind, and the surface has a glassy look. That degree of evenness at this size and saturation is more characteristic of glass or lab-created material than of a natural gem; natural emerald in particular is almost always included and rarely this clean. Best treated as a probable glass or synthetic green stone unless testing says otherwise.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.
