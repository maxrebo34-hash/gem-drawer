Two Vivid Red Marquise Cuts — Specimen Tray, Matching Pair

A matched pair of saturated red marquise stones; not offered as ruby.

Two marquise-cut stones forming a matched pair, both a vivid saturated red, from the unlabeled specimen tray. Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
