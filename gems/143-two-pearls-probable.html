Two Pearls (Probable) — Specimen Tray, Matched Pair

Two smooth round white-cream beads with the soft sheen of pearl.

Two smooth, round, lustrous white-to-cream beads with no faceting at all — the only pieces in the specimen tray that are plainly not cut stones. The soft characteristic sheen reads as pearl. Whether they are natural, cultured or imitation cannot be told from a photograph: genuine pearls, cultured pearls and glass or shell-bead imitations all look similar, and only testing or expert inspection separates them. Offered as a probable matched pair of pearls.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.
