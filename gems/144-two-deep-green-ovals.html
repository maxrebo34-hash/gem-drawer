Two Deep Green Ovals — Specimen Tray, Possibly Glass or Synthetic

A matched pair of vivid green ovals, unusually clean for their saturation.

Two oval-cut stones forming a matched pair, both a vivid deep green. The same pattern applies as with the other intensely saturated, flawless-looking stones in this tray: the color is extremely even with no visible inclusions or zoning, which is more characteristic of glass or lab-created material than of natural emerald, which is almost always included. Best treated as probable glass or synthetic pending inspection.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.
