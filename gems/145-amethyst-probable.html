Catalogue no. 145

Amethyst (Probable) — Large Vivid Purple Pear

A large purple pear showing natural color zoning — a reassuring sign.

A large pear-cut stone in a vivid purple, and one of the more confident calls in the unlabeled specimen tray. Unlike the suspiciously flawless stones nearby, this one shows visible color zoning — deeper purple concentrated toward the crown, lighter toward the tip — which is a classic feature of natural amethyst and is difficult to reproduce in glass or most synthetics. A good visual match for amethyst, though uncertified.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads.
