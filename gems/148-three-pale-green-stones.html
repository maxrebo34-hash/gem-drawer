Catalogue no. 148

Three Pale Green Stones — Specimen Tray, Mixed Forms

A tumbled slab, a small faceted oval and a rough round, all pale green.

Three stones in a similar pale-to-medium green but three different forms: a large tumbled rectangular piece with a smooth polished surface but no faceting, a small faceted oval, and a small rough or tumbled round. Two of the three are unfaceted rough rather than cut gems. Fluorite, prehnite, beryl and chrysoprase all occupy this pale green range. Offered as an unidentified mixed group.
About this stone: it comes from a twenty-five compartment specimen tray that arrived with no labels or paperwork of any kind. Everything said about it here is read from the photographs alone.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads.
