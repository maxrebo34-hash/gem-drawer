Cat's Eye Chrysoberyl — Labeled Alexandrite, 1.10 ct

A dark oval cabochon with a clear chatoyant eye; the alexandrite claim is unverified.

An oval cabochon labeled “1.10 ct Cat's Eye Alexandrite,” showing a distinct chatoyant eye line across a dark grey-black body. An important caveat is owed here: genuine cat's-eye alexandrite is among the rarest gem materials in existence and also among the most frequently imitated, and the color change alexandrite should show — green in daylight, red-purple under incandescent light — is not visible in these photographs. This stone carries no lab report and should be treated as unverified. It is offered as labeled, not as certified alexandrite.
