Catalogue no. 17

Andesine — 1.80 ct Emerald Cut, Color-Change

A 9 x 7 mm emerald cut flashing green and copper-red by turns.

A 9 x 7 mm emerald-cut andesine labeled at 1.80 carats, showing a distinct shift between green and copper-red-orange depending on viewing angle. Buyers should know that color-change andesine of this appearance has a well-documented history on the gem market of being produced by copper diffusion treatment, a subject of trade controversy since the mid-2000s. This stone is therefore offered as possibly treated rather than as confirmed natural color, and carries no lab report either way.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads.
