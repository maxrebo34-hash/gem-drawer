Catalogue no. 25

Ethiopian Opal — 8 x 10 mm Oval Cabochon

A white-cream cabochon with strong green, orange and blue play-of-color.

An 8 x 10 mm oval opal cabochon with a white to cream body color and strong play-of-color flashing green, orange and blue — an appearance consistent with Ethiopian Welo opal, as the label states. No carat weight is printed on the label. Please note that Ethiopian opal is typically hydrophane, meaning it is porous and can absorb water or oils in ways that temporarily or permanently alter its color and clarity; it needs careful handling. The label's wording is plural, but only one stone is present in the box.
