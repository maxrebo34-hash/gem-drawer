Bixbite (Red Beryl) — Emerald Cut, As Labeled

A small deep red emerald cut labeled as red beryl — rare material, unverified.

A small deep red to maroon emerald-cut stone, labeled “Bixbite” — the trade name for red beryl, a genuinely rare material mined almost exclusively at a couple of Utah localities and sometimes marketed as “red emerald.” Because red beryl carries a substantial value premium and is sometimes confused with or substituted by pyrope garnet or synthetic material, this stone would benefit from laboratory verification and is offered strictly as labeled. The label's own figures are stock wording — “.25 ct min” and “mm varies” — rather than measurements taken from this particular stone.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
