Pale Oval — Labeled Natural Tanzanite, 1.10 ct

Labeled tanzanite at 5.5 x 7.5 mm, but the stone photographs near-colorless.

A 5.5 x 7.5 mm oval labeled “1.10 ct Natural Tanzanite.” There is a mismatch worth stating plainly: tanzanite is known for a strong blue-violet color, and this stone appears essentially colorless to very pale grey in the photographs, with no blue or violet visible. That could mean a genuine but near-colorless, lightly toned tanzanite — these do exist — or a labeling mix-up, or a different colorless material such as white topaz, white sapphire or zircon. It is offered as labeled, with the discrepancy disclosed.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
