Catalogue no. 29

Spinel Crystals — Rough Lot of ~11–12 Pieces

A dozen small raw purple-mauve crystals, uncut natural form.

A lot of roughly eleven to twelve small raw spinel crystals in purple to mauve, each around 3–6 mm, in natural uncut crystal form rather than faceted. This is rough specimen or crafting material, not finished cut gemstones. Because the pieces are unpolished, identification from appearance alone is less certain than with a cut stone — the octahedral habit and color are consistent with spinel but are not independently confirmed. The label gives a novelty product name and no weight or measurements.
