Petalite — Pair of 9 x 11 mm Ovals

Two colorless oval petalite — an uncommon gem in the retail trade.

A pair of colorless oval petalite measuring 9 x 11 mm each. Faceted gem-quality petalite is something of a specialty item, since most petalite production goes to industrial and ceramics use rather than to jewelry. The box holds two stones, and the label's 9.10 ct figure does not specify whether it is per stone or for the pair.
