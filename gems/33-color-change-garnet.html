Catalogue no. 33

Color-Change Garnet — 1.92 ct Oval

A 6 x 7.5 mm oval labeled color-change; photographs brownish-pink.

A 6 x 7.5 mm oval garnet labeled as color-change at 1.92 carats. Color-change garnet is a genuinely sought-after material — associated with certain Madagascar and East African deposits — that shifts between a greenish or brownish tone in daylight and a pinkish red under incandescent light. That shift cannot be demonstrated in a single photograph taken under one light source, so the color change is offered as labeled rather than as verified. In these images the stone reads brownish-pink.
