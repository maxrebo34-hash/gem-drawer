Catalogue no. 35

Color-Change Garnet — Pair of Rounds

Two round garnets labeled color-change; one reads pinker, one more amber.

A pair of round garnets labeled “C/C garnet” — color-change — with the box's 1.40 ct figure not specifying whether it applies per stone or to the pair. As with the other color-change garnet in this collection, the shift cannot be confirmed from photographs under a single light source. Worth noting honestly: under the same lighting the two stones already read somewhat differently from one another, one more pink-mauve and the other more golden-amber. That may simply be each catching the light at a different angle, but they should not be assumed to be a closely matched pair.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
