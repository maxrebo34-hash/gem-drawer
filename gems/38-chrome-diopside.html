Chrome Diopside — Round Mixed Cut

A deep green chrome diopside, a chromium-colored gem in the pyroxene family.

A single deep green chrome diopside in a round-to-oval mixed cut. Chrome diopside is a well-established and readily available green gemstone, colored by chromium, usually found in smaller sizes with good clarity. Please note it is moderately soft at roughly 5.5 to 6 on the Mohs scale and is sensitive to high heat and harsh cleaning, so it wants gentle handling. No weight or measurements were printed on the label.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads.
