Demantoid Garnet — Rough Crystal, As Labeled

A small uncut olive-green crystal labeled demantoid.

A single small raw olive-green crystal, uncut, labeled as demantoid garnet. Demantoid is among the most valuable garnet varieties, prized for its fire and historically associated with Russia's Ural Mountains and more recently Namibia and Iran. Two honest caveats: identifying a small rough crystal from photographs is markedly less certain than identifying a faceted stone, and demantoid is easily confused with ordinary green garnet in rough form. This is offered as labeled, unverified rough material.
