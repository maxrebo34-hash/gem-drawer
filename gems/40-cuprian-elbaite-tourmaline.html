Cuprian Elbaite Tourmaline — Pale Blue Trillion

Copper-bearing tourmaline — the Paraiba family — but in a pale, not neon, blue.

A small pale blue trillion-cut stone labeled “cuprian elbaite,” the gemological name for copper-bearing tourmaline — the same material family as Paraiba tourmaline. The distinction matters for a buyer: fine Paraiba commands extraordinary prices for its saturated neon blue-green, and this stone is a light, pale blue rather than that vivid color. Pale Paraiba-type material from Nigeria, Mozambique and lower-saturation Brazilian sources is real and collectible but sells for a fraction of top-grade material. Offered as labeled, without lab verification.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
