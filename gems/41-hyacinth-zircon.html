Hyacinth Zircon — 4 ct Marquise

A deep reddish-brown marquise; “hyacinth” is the old trade name for this color.

A marquise-cut zircon in deep reddish-brown, labeled at 4 carats. “Hyacinth,” also spelled jacinth, is a genuine traditional trade name for reddish-orange to red-brown zircon rather than a separate species — a term worth knowing if it is unfamiliar. Natural zircon is commonly heat-treated to improve color, and it is somewhat brittle, with facet edges that can chip if handled roughly. This box's information is handwritten rather than printed.
