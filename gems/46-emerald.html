Catalogue no. 46

Emerald — 1.15 ct Emerald Cut, As Labeled

A pale green 7.5 x 5.5 mm step cut with the fine inclusions typical of emerald.

A 7.5 x 5.5 mm emerald-cut stone labeled as emerald at 1.15 carats, showing the fine internal inclusions the trade calls jardin — characteristic of natural emerald rather than a defect. Two things a buyer should weigh: nearly all natural emeralds are clarity-enhanced with oil or resin, a long-accepted practice that nonetheless affects care and should be assumed here; and the color reads fairly pale, closer to what the trade would classify as green beryl than as emerald-grade green. With lab-created emeralds and glass imitations both common, this stone is offered as labeled and unverified.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
