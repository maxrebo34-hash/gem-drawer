Catalogue no. 51

Common Opal — 3.23 ct Lavender Cabochon

A translucent lavender cabochon with no play-of-color.

A smooth polished oval cabochon in solid translucent lavender-purple. Described plainly: this stone shows no play-of-color — none of the rainbow flashes that define precious opal, including the Ethiopian Welo material its label names. It is more consistent with common opal, sometimes called potch, which is a legitimate natural material but carries different value and buyer expectations. Ethiopian opal is often hydrophane and can craze or crack with temperature change or prolonged water exposure, so careful handling applies regardless. The label gives the weight as a range, 3.00 to 3.46 ct.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
