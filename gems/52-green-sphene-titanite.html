Green Sphene (Titanite) — Pair of 7 mm Rounds

Two yellowish-green rounds of a specialty gem with more fire than diamond.

A pair of 7 mm round green sphene, also known as titanite — a lesser-known specialty gem prized among collectors for exceptionally high dispersion, meaning it throws more spectral fire than diamond does. It is relatively soft and wants gentle handling. The box holds two stones and the label's 1.50 ct figure does not specify whether it is per stone or for the pair.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
