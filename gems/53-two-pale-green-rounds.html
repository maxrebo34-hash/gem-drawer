Two Pale Green Rounds — No Label, Unidentified

A matched pair of mint-green rounds with no label information of any kind.

Two round pale mint-green stones that arrived with no usable documentation at all — no printed label, no barcode, only an illegible handwritten note. Material, weight and measurements are all unknown. Pale green rounds of this appearance could be any of several species: peridot, green sapphire, mint tourmaline, tsavorite or demantoid garnet, or green quartz among others. Rather than guess, this pair is listed honestly as unidentified and would benefit from a gemologist's opinion.
Please read: the paperwork that came with this stone does not describe what is in the box, or is missing entirely. It is catalogued by appearance and offered without a material identification.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.
