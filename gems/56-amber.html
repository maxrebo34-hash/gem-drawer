Catalogue no. 56

Amber — 36.94 ct Polished Freeform

A translucent orange amber piece with internal flow lines and inclusions.

An irregular polished and tumbled piece of amber, translucent orange with visible internal flow lines and inclusions typical of natural material, noted at 36.94 carats on a handwritten tag. Amber is fossilized tree resin — an organic gem rather than a mineral — and is very soft at roughly 2 to 2.5 on the Mohs scale. It scratches easily and is sensitive to heat and solvents, so ultrasonic cleaners, perfume and hairspray should be kept away from it. The amber market includes pressed “ambroid” and plastic or copal imitations that are hard to distinguish without testing, so this piece is offered untested.
