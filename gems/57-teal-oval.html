Catalogue no. 57

Teal Oval — Two Conflicting Labels, Unidentified

A teal blue-green oval whose box carries two labels, neither matching the stone.

A teal to blue-green oval-cut stone accompanied by two pieces of paperwork that contradict both each other and the stone itself. The printed label reads “4.00 ct Hyacinth Zircon, 9 mm round,” but hyacinth zircon is a warm orange to red-brown color and this stone is neither that color nor round. A separate handwritten note reads “10 ct Nickel Topaz,” which fits no better. With no documentation that actually describes what is in the box, this stone is offered as unidentified — it could be tourmaline, sapphire or zircon in an atypical color, among others.
Please read: the paperwork that came with this stone does not describe what is in the box, or is missing entirely. It is catalogued by appearance and offered without a material identification.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
