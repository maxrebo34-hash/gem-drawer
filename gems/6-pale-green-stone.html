Pale Green Stone — Labeled “Swastone,” 4.90 ct

Mint-green cushion/shield cut with a notched corner; label name is not a recognized gem term.

A pale mint-green stone in a cushion or shield cut with a distinctive notched top corner, handwritten on its label as “Swastone” at 4.90 carats. “Swastone” is not a recognized gem species or trade name, so the material here is genuinely unidentified — the name is reproduced only because that is what the seller wrote. A buyer should treat this as an unidentified stone pending professional identification. The same handwritten label also carries a $604.00 figure, most likely the original purchase price.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
