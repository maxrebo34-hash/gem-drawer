Padparadscha Sapphire — 1.50 ct Oval, As Labeled

A pale pinkish-peach 5.5 x 8 mm oval labeled padparadscha.

A 5.5 x 8 mm oval sapphire in a pale pinkish-peach, labeled directly as padparadscha at 1.50 carats. Padparadscha is the rare and highly prized pink-orange sapphire variety; its grading is famously subjective, with no single agreed color standard between laboratories, and this stone sits on the lighter, more pastel end of the range. Because the price gap between a true padparadscha and an ordinary peach sapphire is wide, it is offered as labeled and without a lab report.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
