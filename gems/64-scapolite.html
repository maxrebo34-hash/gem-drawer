Scapolite — 4.99 ct Round Brilliant

A pale champagne round brilliant of an uncommon collector's gem.

A round brilliant-cut scapolite in pale champagne-yellow, just under five carats. Scapolite is a lesser-known gem that occurs naturally in yellow, pink, purple and colorless forms; the champagne tone here is among its most typical colors. Some scapolite shows a cat's-eye effect when cut as a cabochon or fluoresces under ultraviolet light, though this piece is a conventional faceted brilliant. A good size for the material.
