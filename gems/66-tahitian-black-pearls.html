Catalogue no. 66

Tahitian Black Pearls — Set of Three 11 mm Rounds

Three loose cultured pearls in dark gunmetal charcoal, 11 mm each.

Three loose round cultured Tahitian pearls, 11 mm each, in a dark gunmetal charcoal grey. These are the only organic gems in the collection alongside the amber, which is why the label gives a diameter rather than a carat weight — pearls are conventionally sized in millimeters. Genuine Tahitian pearls are grown in Pinctada margaritifera oysters, primarily in French Polynesia, and range through grey, black, green and peacock overtones rather than true jet black, as these do. Pearls are soft and sensitive to perfume, cosmetics and chemicals; they need careful storage.
