Green Zircon — 2.20 ct Cushion

An olive-forest green 7 x 8 mm cushion of an uncommon zircon variety.

A 7 x 8 mm cushion-cut green zircon at 2.20 carats, in an olive to forest green. Green zircon is considerably less common than the heat-treated blue and colorless zircon most buyers know: its color typically comes from natural radiation damage accumulated in the crystal over geologic time — the so-called “low” or metamict form — rather than from heat treatment. Zircon can be brittle at the facet edges, so it wants care in handling and setting.
