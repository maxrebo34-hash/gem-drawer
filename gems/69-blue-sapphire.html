Catalogue no. 69

Blue Sapphire — 2.71 ct Oval, Labeled Burmese

A very deep blue 9 x 7 mm oval; the Burmese origin claim is unverified.

A 9 x 7 mm oval blue sapphire at 2.71 carats, in a very deep, dark blue that can read nearly black in some light and opens up considerably in others. Two things stated plainly: a tone this dark is genuine for sapphire but sits at the inky end that some buyers find less desirable than a brighter mid-tone; and Burmese origin, which carries a real value premium, cannot be determined from appearance or a paper label — only a specialized laboratory analyzing trace elements and inclusions can establish it. Heat treatment is near-universal for blue sapphire and should be assumed.
