Catalogue no. 72

Sapphire Cabochon — Labeled Blue Star, Asterism Unconfirmed

A domed grey-blue cabochon labeled star sapphire; no star visible in the photos.

A smooth domed sapphire cabochon in a greyish blue-lavender. The cabochon cut is correct for a star stone — faceting would destroy the effect — but the asterism itself is not visible in these photographs, whether because of the lighting angle or because it is weak. A star sapphire without a visible star is a materially different and less valuable item, so this is offered as a sapphire cabochon with the star claim unconfirmed. Viewing it under a single direct light source is the way to settle the question. No weight is given on the label.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
