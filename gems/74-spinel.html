Catalogue no. 74

Spinel — Mixed Pair, Labeled Burmese

Two oval spinels of different colors: near-colorless grey and greyish mauve.

Two oval spinels of visibly different colors — one near-colorless to pale grey, one greyish purple-mauve — which is what the “mix” on the label refers to. The 1.15 ct figure is marked with a plus sign, suggesting an approximate combined weight for both. As with the blue sapphire elsewhere in this collection, Burmese origin carries a premium and cannot be established from a paper label; it is repeated here as labeled. Spinel is a fine gem in its own right, historically mistaken for ruby and sapphire and long undervalued because of it.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory. Weights and measurements are taken from the labels rather than from a scale or gauge. The collection comes from a single estate and is offered as one complete lot.
