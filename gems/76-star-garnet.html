Star Garnet — Round Cabochon with Visible Asterism

A near-black domed cabochon showing a clear four-rayed star.

A round star garnet cabochon in the very dark, near-black body color typical of the material — star garnets are almost always almandine and run dark red to brownish or purplish black. The asterism here is not a claim: a clear four-rayed star is plainly visible in the closeup photograph. Star garnet is genuinely uncommon; asterism occurs far less often in garnet than in sapphire or ruby, and gem-quality material comes almost exclusively from Idaho and, to a lesser extent, India. One of the more distinctive pieces in the collection. No weight is given on the label.
