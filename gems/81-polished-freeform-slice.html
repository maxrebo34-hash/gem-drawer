Polished Freeform Slice — Label Does Not Match, Unidentified

A brown-grey freeform slice with a ring pattern; the label reads strawberry quartz.

A freeform polished slice, opaque brownish-grey to taupe with a distinctive ring or eye-shaped pattern running through it. The label reads “Strawberry Quartz,” which this is not — strawberry quartz is a translucent pink-to-red material with sparkling reddish inclusions, and this piece matches it in neither color, transparency, nor cut style. The banded pattern and earthy tones suggest an agate or jasper, but that is unconfirmed. Offered as an unidentified polished ornamental stone.
Please read: the paperwork that came with this stone does not describe what is in the box, or is missing entirely. It is catalogued by appearance and offered without a material identification.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
