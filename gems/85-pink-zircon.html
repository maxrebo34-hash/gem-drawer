Pink Zircon — 1.55 ct, 6.5 mm Round

A 6.5 mm round that reads darker than “pink” suggests — closer to maroon.

A 6.5 mm round zircon labeled pink at 1.55 carats. Described accurately, it photographs darker than the name implies: an almost blackish maroon toward the edges with a lighter pink flash near the center. Some of that is a real property of the material — zircon has unusually strong double refraction, which blurs the back facets and can make a stone read darker and softer at certain angles than it does face-up in diffused light. Heat treatment is standard for zircon color.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
