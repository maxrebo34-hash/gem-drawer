Aquamarine — Pair of Trillions, Very Pale

Two trillion cuts labeled aquamarine that photograph essentially colorless.

A pair of trillion-cut stones labeled aquamarine at 1.55 carats combined. Stated honestly: both photograph essentially colorless, with no blue or blue-green tint visible, which is unusual given that color is aquamarine's defining feature. Very pale aquamarine does exist, especially in smaller stones, and camera lighting can wash out a faint tint — but a truly colorless stone of this shape could equally be goshenite, the colorless member of the same beryl species. Logged as labeled, with the color question flagged.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.
