Catalogue no. 90

Sapphire — Oval, Labeled Midnight Blue

A very dark blue oval sapphire, near blackish-navy in photographs.

A single oval sapphire labeled “midnight blue,” photographing very dark — close to blackish navy, with only a small facet catching the light. That is plausible and even expected for a deeply saturated sapphire, which routinely photographs near black until it is viewed against strong or diffused light, where the blue opens up. The tone is best judged in hand. Heat treatment is common and standard for sapphire. No weight or measurements on the label.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
