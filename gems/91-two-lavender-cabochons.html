Catalogue no. 91

Two Lavender Cabochons — Labeled Star Burma Ruby, Mismatched

Two cabochons labeled star ruby that are lavender-pink, with no clear star.

Two cabochons labeled “Star Burma Ruby” that do not match that description. Ruby is by definition red corundum, and these photograph lavender to purple-pink — a color that would ordinarily make them sapphire, or another material entirely. No clear six-rayed star is visible either; each stone shows a single round white mark that reads more like a reflection or a surface void than true asterism, though a weak star can be hard to capture in a still photograph. The Burmese origin claim is likewise unverifiable from a label. The label's 1.00–1.75 ct range is not broken down per stone.
Please read: the paperwork that came with this stone does not describe what is in the box, or is missing entirely. It is catalogued by appearance and offered without a material identification.
