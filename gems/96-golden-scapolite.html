Catalogue no. 96

Golden Scapolite — Matched Set of Four 7 mm Rounds

Four evenly matched pale champagne rounds — the best-matched set here.

Four round brilliant golden scapolites, 7 mm each, in a consistent pale yellow-champagne. Of the four-stone sets in this collection, this is the most evenly matched in both color and cut — it reads as a genuinely calibrated set and would suit a jewelry commission well. Scapolite is a lesser-known but entirely legitimate gem species, and the golden variety is among its better-known colors; it is not commonly treated. The label's 1.19 ct figure is written in the singular.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads.
