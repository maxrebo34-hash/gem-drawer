Color-Change Garnet — Round

A round garnet labeled color-change; the shift can't be shown in one photograph.

A single round color-change garnet — one of the genuinely rare and desirable varieties in this collection, alongside the star garnet. The effect is a visible shift between light sources, typically bluish-green under daylight or fluorescent light and purplish-red to pink under incandescent, similar in kind to alexandrite. No single photograph under one light source can capture that, and here the stone reads a dark greyish brown, which neither confirms nor contradicts the label. Worth viewing under two light sources. No weight given.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads.
