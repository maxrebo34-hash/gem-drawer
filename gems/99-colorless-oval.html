Colorless Oval — Labeled Aquamarine, 2.09 ct

A 10 x 11 mm oval labeled aquamarine that photographs colorless.

A 10 x 11 mm oval labeled aquamarine at 2.09 carats. It is flagged here as a mismatch: aquamarine is defined by its blue to blue-green color and this stone photographs essentially colorless. A truly colorless stone of this size and shape could be goshenite — the colorless member of the same beryl species — or another material entirely. Very pale aquamarine does exist and camera flash can wash out faint color, so the question is worth settling in natural light, but it is not offered as confirmed aquamarine.

Every stone in this collection is described exactly as its original box label reads. Where a label disagrees with what is in the box, where a name is a seller's own coinage rather than a recognised gemological term, or where a stone carries no label at all, that is stated plainly on the item's page.

Nothing here has been independently graded, certified or appraised, and no origin, treatment or variety claim printed on a label has been verified by a laboratory.
